ABOUT THE PROJECT

AI Based Healthcare System & AI Medikit Guider

A technology-driven project focused on improving healthcare awareness and accessibility.

Our Vision

Healthcare accessibility remains a major challenge in many rural and semi-urban areas. People may not always have immediate access to doctors or reliable healthcare guidance.

The AI Based Healthcare System & AI Medikit Guider is designed as a smart digital healthcare assistant that supports preliminary guidance, healthcare awareness, medicine understanding, and responsible decision-making.

The purpose is not to replace doctors, but to help users understand when they may need professional medical support.
